The Seven-Book Journey

A quick, spoiler-light look at the series' arc — from a cupboard under the stairs to the Battle of Hogwarts. Original summaries written for this project.

Book One

1997

An orphaned boy discovers on his eleventh birthday that he's a wizard — and that a hidden school, a talking hat, and an old rivalry are waiting for him.

Book Two

1998

Whispers of a hidden chamber and a monster of legend put the school on edge, and our hero must untangle a fifty-year-old mystery before it's too late.

Book Three

1999

An escaped prisoner is said to be hunting for him, but the truth behind the escape — and a secret about his parents' past — is far stranger than the rumors.

Book Four

2000

A legendary inter-school tournament, an uninvited fourth champion, and a graveyard at the end of a maze — the series turns darker here.

Book Five

2003

Nobody believes him, the Ministry is closing in on the school, and a secret defense group becomes the only place left to learn the truth.

Book Six

2005

A mysterious old textbook, private lessons on the enemy's past, and a shocking loss set the stage for the final confrontation.

Book Seven

2007

No school, no safety net — just a scavenger hunt across the country for the objects that could finally end the war, and a final battle to defend the place it all began.

Looking to read them? Your local library or bookshop is the best place to start — try Open Library to find an edition near you.

The Four Houses

Every student is sorted into one of four houses at the school, each prizing a different virtue. Here's an original take on each.

G

The Crimson House

Courage & Daring

Home to the bold and the brave-hearted — those who leap first and ask questions later, for better or worse.

S

The Emerald House

Ambition & Cunning

Home to the driven and the resourceful — those who plan three moves ahead and rarely settle for second place.

R

The Bronze House

Wit & Wisdom

Home to the curious and the clever — those who'd rather solve the riddle than skip to the answer.

H

The Golden House

Loyalty & Patience

Home to the steady and the fair — those who show up for their friends long after everyone else has left.

About This Project

Why Potter-Verse?

This site began as a hands-on way to practice building interactive, content-driven front-end pages — carousels, grids, quizzes, and responsive layouts — around a subject with genuine staying power.

An earlier version of this project hosted downloadable book files and reused promotional artwork; that version has been retired. This rebuild uses only original writing, layouts, and CSS/SVG-drawn visuals — no scanned pages, no cover art, no official logos or crests.
